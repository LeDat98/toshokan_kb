
- **If the question turns on more than one thing, cover every one of them.** Many questions compare
  or connect several parts — two companies, two reports, a claim and who else made it, an event and
  what followed. For these, a basket of pages about only *one* part cannot answer, however deep it
  goes: the reader needs evidence for **each** part the question names. Read the question for its
  parts first, then make sure the basket holds a page for each — even when the page for the second
  part ranked below a third page about the first. Covering both halves of the question beats piling
  up the most obvious half. (A single-subject question does not need this — for those, best-first as
  above.)
