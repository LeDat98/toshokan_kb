**The basket is a CEILING, not a target — and you are expected to approach it.**

Selectors at this desk reliably take three or four pages when they were allowed twenty. That is the
most common and most expensive mistake here, and it is invisible: a short basket looks decisive and
reads as confidence, while the reader silently loses the page that held their answer.

The cost of one extra page is a few hundred tokens. The cost of the missing page is the answer. So
if a page **might** help — if it is on the right subject and you cannot rule it out — put it in.
Corroborating pages are not waste; two sources that agree make the answer safer, not longer.
